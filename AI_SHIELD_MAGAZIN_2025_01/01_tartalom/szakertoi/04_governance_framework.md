## Enterprise AI governance framework

Szervezeti AI kormányzási keretrendszer építése.

### Framework Komponensek

**1. Policy Templates**
- AI Acceptable Use Policy
- Data Handling Policy
- Model Development Standards
- Incident Response Procedures

**2. Role Definitions (RACI Matrix)**
| Role | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| AI Engineer | Execute | - | CISO | Board |
| CISO | Monitor | Yes | Legal | Exec team |
| DPO | Compliance | Yes | - | Regulators |

**3. Approval Workflows**
```
AI Project Proposal
   ↓
Security Review (CISO) → APPROVED/REJECTED
   ↓
GDPR Impact Assessment (DPO)
   ↓
Budget Approval (CFO)
   ↓
Production Deployment
```

**4. Compliance Tracking**
- ISO 27001 mapping
- GDPR compliance dashboard
- AI Act readiness scorecard

### Quarterly Governance Review
- Policy updates
- Incident retrospectives
- Risk register refresh
- Compliance audit
